  • The German market says "Konzentration", not "nootropic": the English keyword returns one B2B result. PRIMARY EV-meta-000219
  • ~150 live "konzentration gummies" ads in DE; about half of the visible ones are long-form mom-story / ADHD advertorials run through creator pages. PRIMARY EV-meta-000218
  • Kids' focus gummies are bought by mothers 25–44: Novomins targets everyone, Meta delivers ~60% to women 35–44. PRIMARY EV-meta-000201
  • Adult focus products lead with "ohne Koffein" and the afternoon slump. PRIMARY EV-meta-000203 PRIMARY EV-meta-000204
  • Price points: €10.99 (kids L-theanine) to €57.55 (bundle, "was" €95.92). PRIMARY EV-shopify-000202 PRIMARY EV-shopify-000201
  • "Cognitive function" may only be claimed for iodine, iron, zinc (and B5 for mental performance); lion's mane, ginseng, L-theanine have no authorised claim. SECONDARY EV-web-000201
  • An ADHD advertorial claims parents drop Vyvanse/Concerta for a supplement — a regulatory and reputational hazard in this niche. PRIMARY EV-meta-000206
